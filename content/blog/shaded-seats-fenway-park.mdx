## The short answer

At Fenway Park, most daytime shade comes from roofs. For a day game, the Grandstand under the roof and the Pavilion clubs are the reliable shade. The bleachers, the Green Monster seats and the Field Box sit in the sun. Behind home plate and up the third-base line, shade arrives late in the game. For a night game, every seat is out of the sun by the middle innings.

Everything below comes from our shade calculator run on real 2025 Red Sox home games. To see every section inning by inning for your game, pick it on our [Fenway Park page](/stadium/redsox).

## A 1:35 PM game in July

These answers are for July 13, 2025 (1:35 PM vs the Rays), when the sun was high in the south-southwest at first pitch. The ones in the first two lists are rated High confidence: they hold even if the stands are a little taller or shorter than we think.

### Shaded all game

- Grandstand sections 1–33 are under the roof and shaded for the whole game.
- The Pavilion clubs (the Dell Technologies Club and the Aura Club) are shaded for the whole game.

### In the sun all game

- Bleacher sections 34–43 are in the sun for the whole game.
- The Green Monster seats are in the sun for the whole game.
- Roof Box seats are in the sun for the whole game.
- On the first-base side, Field Box 9–41 is in the sun for the whole game.

Field Box seats are in the sun for almost the whole game, all the way around.

### Where shade arrives

Our estimate (Low confidence, because the exact height of the stands behind home plate matters here): the Loge Box behind home plate and up the third-base line, about sections 132–155, gets shade late. Some rows are shaded from about the 6th inning, and most of each section by the 9th.

## A 4:10 PM game

Later starts put the sun further west, behind home plate and the third-base side. On July 12, 2025 (4:10 PM vs the Rays), our estimate is that Field Box 50–61, right behind home plate, are shaded from about the 4th inning. The first-base side (Field Box 9–38 and Loge Box 87–106) stays in the sun.

The Green Monster seats get no shade at all during 1:35 PM or 4:10 PM games in July.

## Night games

On July 11, 2025 (7:10 PM vs the Rays), the sun was low in the west-northwest at first pitch and set during the game. Most Field Box seats are already in shade by the 2nd inning, and by the 6th inning every seat is out of the sun.

## How we worked this out

Our calculator places the sun with the NREL Solar Position Algorithm for the exact first pitch, then checks each row of each section against the stands and roofs around it, every half hour for three hours (a 2025 game averaged 2 hours 38 minutes). Fenway faces northeast: home plate to center field is 44°, measured from the foul lines on USGS aerial photos. Which rows sit under a roof comes from published seating guides, and the Green Monster's 37-foot height from the park's records.

Every answer carries a confidence label. High means it stays the same if the park is turned a degree either way and the stands are somewhat taller or shorter than our estimates. Low means changes that small could flip it. Where we're not sure, we say so.
