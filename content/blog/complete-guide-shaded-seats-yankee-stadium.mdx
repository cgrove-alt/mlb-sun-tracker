## The short answer

For a summer afternoon game at Yankee Stadium, shade comes from overhead: the deck above you or the roof over the upper deck. Seats in the open stay in the sun all game. In the upper deck, shade starts on the first-base side and works around behind home plate as the sun moves west. For a night game, the whole ballpark is out of the sun by the middle innings.

Everything below comes from our shade calculator run on real 2025 Yankees home games. To see every section inning by inning for your game, pick it on our [Yankee Stadium page](/stadium/yankees).

## A 1:05 PM game in July

These answers are for July 12, 2025 (1:05 PM vs the Cubs), when the sun was nearly due south and about 70° up at first pitch. Each one is rated High confidence unless we say otherwise: it holds even if the stands are a little taller or shorter than we measured.

### In the sun all game

- Bleacher sections 202–204 and 235–238 are in the sun for the whole game.
- Field level sections 103, 104, 135 and 136 are in the sun for the whole game.
- Terrace sections 305–309 and 332–334 are in the sun for the whole game.
- The Legends seats (sections 011–029) are in the sun through the 9th inning (Medium confidence for most of them).

### Partly shaded

- Main level sections 205–207 and 232–234 are partly shaded all game: the back rows sit under the deck above, the front rows get sun.
- Sections 208–213 and 228–231 are the same through the 7th inning (Low confidence: the exact height of the deck above matters there).
- Most field level sections between 105 and 129 are the same through the 7th inning: partly shaded, with the front rows in the sun. By the 9th, about one in four is fully shaded.

If you want shade at field or main level, ask for a row near the back of the section.

### Where shade arrives

The roof over the Grandstand (400s) gives the most shade in the ballpark. Almost half the 400-level sections are shaded at first pitch and nearly three in four by the end of the game.

The shade moves around the upper deck with the sun (Medium confidence): sections 410–419 on the first-base side are shaded from first pitch, the sections right behind home plate (420–421) by the 2nd inning, and 422–430 between the 4th and the 7th.

## Night games

On July 10, 2025 (7:05 PM vs the Mariners), the sun was low in the west-northwest at first pitch and set during the game. By the 2nd inning, almost every section in the 100s and 200s is in shade, and by the 6th inning the whole ballpark is.

## April and September

The sun is lower in spring and early fall, so shade arrives earlier. On April 13, 2025 (1:35 PM vs the Giants), a third of the terrace sections were shaded at first pitch and three in four by the end of the game; on July 12 none of them were shaded until the 6th inning.

## How we worked this out

Our calculator places the sun with the NREL Solar Position Algorithm for the exact first pitch, then checks each row of each section against the decks and roof around it, every half hour for three hours (a 2025 game averaged 2 hours 38 minutes). Yankee Stadium faces east-northeast: home plate to center field is 75°, measured from USGS aerial photos. The heights of the stands and the roof canopy are measured from USGS airborne lidar of the ballpark (2014); the canopy's height matches New York City's building records.

Every answer carries a confidence label. High means it stays the same if the stadium is turned a degree either way and the decks are somewhat taller or shorter than our measurements. Low means changes that small could flip it. Where we're not sure, we say so.
