# Coors Field geometry records request draft

Status: Draft only. Not submitted. External communication requires explicit user authorization.

## Evidence already established

- Denver e-permits record `2013-LOG-M000304`, building log `2013M00304`, is titled `2013M00304 - 2001 Blake St - Coors Field 2013 improvements - 301-310 project` and is `Closed - Approved`.
- The record's intake comments say `Collected & sent Assessor plans. 10/2/13 dcm` and list electrical, mechanical/plumbing, health, and plumbing resubmittals from November 2013 through January 2014.
- Denver's exact-address permit search was queried in four non-overlapping date partitions from January 1, 1990 through August 11, 2026. The fail-closed crawl recovered 985 source rows, 981 canonical records, and four audited duplicate rows.
- Seventeen building-log records were identified as priority geometry targets because their official project names describe seating, specific seating sections, grandstand removal, suites, shade structures, shelters, canopies, a covered entry, a scoreboard, or the press box.
- Denver record `2019-LOG-0000784`, titled `2001 BLAKE ST - COORS FIELD - NEW SHADE STRUCTURES`, is `Closed - Approved`. Its application information records `Electronic Plans: Yes`, a construction valuation of `$98,000`, and a tenant name of `COLORADO ROCKIES`. The anonymous public record exposes no attachment tab.
- The June 17, 2013 official stadium-district minutes say Joe Spear of Populous presented plans for renovating Sections 301 through 310. The board approved the concept and adopted Resolution 7-13.
- The public e-permits detail page exposes no attachment control. The stadium district's current site and 697 distinct archived official-site URLs expose no named plan set.
- Two official lease packages and one geotechnical RFP, 278 pages total, were visually reviewed. Their stadium exhibits are blank title sheets, and the only recovered plan covers the separate west parking lot.
- Section 26.2 of the district's official 2017 Baseball Stadium Lease Agreement expressly states that the district owns the architectural drawings of the stadium and stadium land. The district is therefore the primary known custodian for the original geometry records, not merely a possible routing contact.
- The district's complete public search index was queried with record deduplication disabled on August 10, 2026. It returned 111 indexed records across 53 unique public content objects. The seven files posted under Legal & Financials are budgets, audited financials, the 2017 lease, and a lease summary. No drawing, CAD, BIM, as-built, row-tread, riser, canopy, or overhang package is posted.
- The district's official June 12, 2024 minutes state that MLB requirements, engineering reports, and the Martin/Martin study guide the capital work requested each year.
- The district's official November 13, 2024 minutes state that the Rockies work from a 30-year plan developed with the district and regularly updated by Martin & Martin. The study, plan, revisions, and underlying engineering exhibits are therefore specific additional records targets for current geometry.
- The district's official July 22, 2022 minutes identify ongoing concrete repairs, steel coatings, ceiling-joint work, backstop-netting repair, suite renovation, and other capital work. Current exact-shade geometry must therefore be checked against the final record drawings for every later project that changed seating tiers or shadow-casting structures.

## Request 2: Denver Metropolitan Major League Baseball Stadium District

Suggested channel: the district's official contact form at `https://www.dmmlbsd.com/contact-us`. The district also publishes 2195 Blake Street, Denver, CO 80205 and 303-292-0200.

Suggested subject: CORA request for Coors Field architectural drawings, surveyed seating geometry, and current obstruction records

Draft text:

> Under the Colorado Open Records Act, I request electronic inspection copies of records defining the seating and overhead geometry of Coors Field. Section 26.2 of the district's official 2017 Baseball Stadium Lease Agreement states that the district owns the architectural drawings of the stadium and stadium land.
>
> The district's official June 17, 2013 meeting minutes state that Joe Spear of Populous presented plans for a new concept for Sections 301 through 310, including removal of approximately 3,000 permanent seats, movable seating and standing-room areas, gathering areas, and two restaurant/bar tiers. The board approved the concept and adopted Resolution 7-13.
>
> Please provide:
>
> 1. The complete final architectural and structural construction-document set for the original stadium, including every drawing index, addendum, bulletin, field revision, shop drawing incorporated into the record set, and final as-built or record drawing.
> 2. Survey control, project benchmarks, horizontal and vertical datums, layout points, and civil or architectural control records needed to place the stadium geometry in a surveyed three-dimensional coordinate frame.
> 3. Seating-bowl plans, sections, elevations, and details for every ticket-addressable section and row, including section and row labels, row-center or seat-anchor positions, tread depths, riser heights, slab and precast profiles, portals, guardrails, fascia, railings, and absolute floor, tread, or seat elevations.
> 4. Plans, sections, elevations, details, schedules, and structural records defining every surface capable of casting a shadow on spectator seating, including upper-deck overhangs, fixed canopies, roofs, soffits, fascia, catwalks, press-level projections, scoreboards, and the measured underside and edge geometry of each surface.
> 5. Resolution 7-13 in its complete executed form, including every attachment, exhibit, appendix, and incorporated plan, plus the complete board packet, presentation, drawings, renderings, and plan set presented by Joe Spear or Populous for the June 17, 2013 meeting.
> 6. Approved schematic-design, design-development, construction-document, permit, addendum, bulletin, field-revision, and as-built or record drawing sets for the Sections 301 through 310 renovation.
> 7. Final drawings and survey or closeout records for every later capital repair, renovation, seating change, concrete repair, steel repair or coating project, ceiling or expansion-joint project, backstop-netting project, suite renovation, scoreboard project, or other alteration through the present that changed a seating row or a shadow-casting obstruction.
> 8. The Martin/Martin study identified in the district's June 12, 2024 minutes, every revision or update to that study, and every underlying or incorporated engineering report, inspection, drawing, model, calculation, exhibit, photograph, or repair-priority schedule that defines seating-tier or overhead geometry.
> 9. The 30-year plan identified in the district's November 13, 2024 minutes, every revision or update prepared by or with Martin & Martin, and every drawing, model, engineering report, exhibit, or project package incorporated into or used to update that plan.
> 10. Every district approval, written consent, engineering review, and attached drawing or calculation for an alteration, addition, or improvement to a roof, exterior wall, seating tier, concrete structure, steel structure, canopy, overhang, soffit, fascia, catwalk, press-level projection, scoreboard, or other potential shadow-casting structure.
> 11. Records identifying the final architect, structural engineer, contractor, drawing-set date, revision date, project or permit number, and current records custodian for each supplied package if those identifiers are not included in the drawing sets.
>
> Please provide native electronic files when they exist, including original PDF, CAD, or BIM files. If native files are unavailable, please provide the highest-resolution electronic scans held by the district. Color, scale bars, sheet dimensions, stamps, revision clouds, and title blocks should be preserved.
>
> This request is limited to records that define the physical geometry of the seating tiers and overhead obstructions. It does not seek general correspondence unless an attachment is itself a responsive drawing, calculation, presentation, or record listed above.
>
> If the district does not hold a responsive item but knows that it is maintained by the Colorado Rockies, Populous, a contractor, or another records custodian on the district's behalf, please identify that custodian and the record location. If any responsive record is withheld or redacted, please identify the record and the specific legal basis. Please provide a written cost estimate before incurring fees greater than $50.

## Required handling after receipt

Receiving drawings will not by itself make Coors Field publication-ready. Each supplied file must be checksum-locked, dated, visually reviewed, tied to the current configuration, registered to the accepted NGS and DRCOG horizontal frame, and tested against the existing one-foot horizontal and vertical uncertainty limits. The resulting obstruction model must then pass 30 independent timestamped shadow holdouts across at least three dates and at least 25 degrees of solar-altitude span.
