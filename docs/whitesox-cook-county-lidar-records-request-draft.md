## Draft request

To the Cook County Office of the President FOIA Officer:

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act, I request electronic copies of the following existing records created, received, or maintained under Cook County Contract 2103-08021, LiDAR Data Acquisition. The geographic focus is Rate Field, 333 West 35th Street, Chicago, and Cook County tile identifiers 17258800 and 17508800.

1. The final LiDAR and Mapping Report in PDF format, including its acquisition, processing, accuracy-assessment, and POS-report components.
2. The final Ground Control Survey Report in PDF format.
3. The delivered calibration-control and NVA/VVA checkpoint datasets, including the Esri shapefiles or geodatabase feature classes, all attribute tables, projection files, data dictionaries, and accuracy residuals.
4. The delivered flight-index shapefile or geodatabase, including the mapping between LAS point-source IDs, flight lines or swaths, and acquisition dates and times.
5. The MARS QC PDF reports and other final QC records that address tiles 17258800 and 17508800, including boresight, swath-separation, relative-accuracy, absolute-accuracy, and completeness results.
6. The Swath Separation Image tiles and Maximum Surface Height Raster tiles for 17258800 and 17508800.
7. Any delivered ground-control feature class, control-point coordinate table, or project-control table not already included in items 2 or 3.
8. Any final record that states the achieved horizontal accuracy at 95% confidence for the classified LAS deliverable. This includes any record explaining the `ldrchacc` value of 0.175 metres in `cook_2022_metadata.xml` relative to the contract requirement of 3.8 feet at 95% confidence.
9. Any final acceptance, QA/QC assessment, or delivery-inventory record identifying whether the records above were accepted and where they are maintained.

Please provide the records in their native electronic formats when feasible. For shapefiles, please include every component file. For geodatabases, please provide a ZIP archive. I am not asking the County to create a new record or conduct a new analysis.

If any record contains exempt material, please redact only the exempt material and release the reasonably segregable remainder. If a requested record is maintained by another Cook County office, please identify the custodian or forward the request if your procedures permit.

To reduce burden, the raster and tile-specific portions of this request are limited to tiles 17258800 and 17508800. The reports, control datasets, checkpoint datasets, flight index, and delivery inventory are requested at project scope because their accuracy and provenance cannot be evaluated from two clipped tiles alone.

Please provide a written fee estimate before incurring any charge. No fee, physical-media purchase, or paid reproduction is authorized by this draft.

Please make records available on a rolling basis if that would accelerate production.

Sincerely,

`[REQUESTER NAME]`

## Evidence supporting the scope

- Cook County Contract 2103-08021 states a horizontal accuracy requirement of 3.8 feet at 95% confidence and a vertical accuracy requirement of 0.6 feet at 95% confidence.
- The same contract identifies the LiDAR and Mapping Report, Ground Control Survey Report, calibration and checkpoint shapefiles, flight index, MARS QC reports, Maximum Surface Height Raster, and Swath Separation Image as deliverables.
- The public Illinois Height Modernization download exposes the classified LAS and summary metadata but does not expose those control and QA deliverables.
