# Daikin Park geometry records request draft

Status: local draft only. Not submitted. No fee, account creation, personal disclosure, or external communication is authorized.

Primary recipient: Harris County-Houston Sports Authority through its [Texas Public Information Act request process](https://houstonsports.org/texas-public-information-act-requests/).

Secondary recipient if the authority does not hold permitting records: City of Houston Public Information Request Center and the Houston Permitting Center.

## Draft request

Subject: Existing Daikin Park record drawings and current as-built geometry records

Under the Texas Public Information Act, I request copies of existing records for the current ballpark known as Daikin Park and formerly known as Minute Maid Park or Enron Field. The requested period is June 17, 1998 through the date this request is received.

This request seeks existing records only. It does not ask the authority to create a new drawing, conduct measurements, perform research, or answer questions.

The authority's June 17, 1998 Project Agreement identifies relevant record classes. Section 8.6 states that the Project Contractor delivered marked drawings to the Sports Authority. The agreement defines Project Plans to include concept drawings, schematic drawings, design-development drawings, detailed working drawings and specifications, and Final Drawings and Specifications. It also defines Project Submission Matters to include Project Plans, design criteria, design packages, construction documents, construction contract change orders, changes in scope or systems, and changes involving the retractable roof.

Please provide the following existing records, including the current or most recent version and superseded versions when necessary to identify changes:

1. The marked drawings delivered under Section 8.6 of the Project Agreement, together with every existing revision, addendum, as-built drawing, record drawing, field-marked drawing, and final drawing for the ballpark.

2. Project Plans, Final Drawings and Specifications, design criteria, design packages, construction documents, construction contract change orders, architect supplemental instructions, requests for information with incorporated drawing changes, shop drawings, fabrication drawings, and field-survey records that establish the physical geometry of any current shadow-casting surface.

3. Current seating-bowl plans and schedules that identify every section, row label, row centerline or front edge, tread, riser, landing, cross-aisle, accessible platform, standing-room area, suite or club exterior seating area, seat location, and section boundary. Please include coordinate tables, dimension strings, typical sections, and vertical profiles where they exist.

4. Current drawings and existing digital models for all roofs, upper decks, overhangs, canopies, soffits, fascias, scoreboards, ribbon boards, signs, lighting structures, walls, railings, bridges, platforms, facades, columns, beams, trusses, supports, and other exterior or seating-bowl structures that can block direct sunlight. Please include top surfaces, front edges, undersides, thicknesses, supports, and elevations where recorded.

5. Current records for the retractable roof and 115-foot moving glass left-field wall, including plan and profile geometry, track alignments, travel limits, permissible or programmed stop positions, open and closed positions, intermediate positions, tolerances, survey control, and current as-built modifications. This request does not seek security credentials, control-system passwords, source code, or sensitive operational procedures.

6. Current records for the left-field Home Run Train and track, including the train and tender envelope, track alignment and elevation, operating limits, parked position, and the 2025 refurbishment that added 25 baseballs to the tender.

7. Drawings, permits, submittals, shop drawings, field measurements, and final inspection or completion records for the display work installed for the 2023 season, including the main outfield display, ribbon boards, center-field mezzanine displays, supports, attachments, and related structures.

8. Drawings, permits, submittals, shop drawings, and final records for changes associated with the Daikin Park naming-rights transition effective January 1, 2025, including new or modified exterior signs, interior bowl signs, roof signs, support structures, attachments, and any related physical amenity work.

9. Records of seating, roof, moving-wall, scoreboard, signage, overhang, upper-deck, facade, train, or other structural additions, removals, replacements, repairs, capital projects, and field changes completed after February 18, 2024 or still in progress.

10. Existing surveys, control networks, control-point descriptions, benchmarks, coordinate lists, point clouds, photogrammetry, laser scans, inspection scans, deformation surveys, and geometry quality-control reports for the ballpark. Please include the horizontal and vertical datum, coordinate reference system, units, geoid model, survey date, responsible surveyor, and stated accuracy when recorded.

11. Existing CAD, BIM, GIS, spreadsheet, point-cloud, and other native digital files associated with the records above. Preferred formats include DWG, DXF, DGN, RVT, IFC, NWD, NWC, LandXML, SHP, GeoPackage, GeoJSON, CSV, LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, OBJ, PDF, TIFF, and JPEG. Please preserve native coordinates, units, layer names, object identifiers, metadata, and revision history where available.

For each responsive digital file, please provide available metadata that identifies its title, author or creator, creation date, revision date, revision identifier, project number, sheet number, coordinate system, units, and superseded or current status.

If a record contains exempt or security-sensitive material, please provide the segregable non-exempt portion, such as physical dimensions and coordinates with control credentials or sensitive operational details redacted. If another public body or records custodian holds a requested item, please identify that custodian if known.

Electronic delivery in native format is preferred. Before incurring any charge, please provide a written itemized estimate and do not exceed a total charge of $0 without written approval.

- [Walter P Moore project description](https://www.walterpmoore.com/projects/daikin-park) confirms the 580-foot retractable roof and 115-foot moving glass wall but does not publish their current metric geometry.
