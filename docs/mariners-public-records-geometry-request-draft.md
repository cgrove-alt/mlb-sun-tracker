# UNSENT DRAFT: T-Mobile Park metric geometry records request

Status: draft only. Not submitted.

This draft is limited to records needed to validate row-level direct-sun and
shade calculations. It does not authorize submission, payment, account
creation, or disclosure of personal contact details.

## Proposed recipients

1. Washington State Major League Baseball Stadium Public Facilities District,
   directed in writing to its Executive Director and public-records contact.
2. Seattle Department of Construction and Inspections through the City Public
   Records Request Center, with approved permit-plan requests routed to the
   SDCI Microfilm Library when permit numbers are identified.

The PFD is the primary request target because it owns T-Mobile Park, reviews
and approves the Mariners' capital plans, reviews capital reimbursements, and
conducts annual facility oversight. SDCI is the secondary custodian for issued
permits, approved plans, inspection records, and project-number discovery.

## Draft A: Washington State Ballpark PFD

Subject: Washington Public Records Act request for current T-Mobile Park
seating, roof, and obstruction geometry

Under the Washington Public Records Act, chapter 42.56 RCW, please provide
electronic copies of the records listed below for T-Mobile Park, 1250 First
Avenue South, Seattle, Washington.

The request is limited to records defining the physical location, elevation,
shape, or current completion status of ticketed seating rows and structures
that can block direct sunlight. It does not seek security-system plans,
communications unrelated to responsive attachments, personal information, or
proprietary business terms.

Please provide:

1. The latest complete architectural, structural, civil, and seating-bowl
   record-drawing or as-built set for T-Mobile Park, including drawing indices,
   revisions, addenda, bulletins, accepted field changes, and closeout records.
2. Current survey control, project benchmarks, horizontal and vertical datums,
   datum realizations, coordinate epochs, units, layout control, and any final
   registered point cloud or laser scan used for facility or capital planning.
3. Plans, sections, elevations, schedules, and details for every
   ticket-addressable section and row, including section and row identifiers,
   seat or row centerlines, tread depths, riser heights, slab and precast
   profiles, deck elevations, aisle geometry, portals, fascia, guardrails, and
   railings.
4. Plans, sections, elevations, details, and structural records defining every
   surface capable of casting a shadow into spectator seating, including upper
   decks, canopies, roofs, soffits, fascia, catwalks, press-level projections,
   scoreboards, and the measured underside and edge of each overhang.
5. Current as-built plans, surveys, control-system position definitions, and
   manufacturer or structural drawings for the retractable roof, including
   panel geometry, tracks, support structures, parked locations, end stops,
   and every approved operational panel position. Please include the physical
   coordinate or encoder definition that distinguishes each state.
6. Final construction and record drawings for the 2022-2023 Press Club and
   Diamond Club projects, including the former press box, indoor and exterior
   Press Club seating, the expanded 13,000-square-foot Diamond Club, the first
   eight rows behind home plate, and the working press-box relocation that
   reduced Terrace Club capacity by 104 seats.
7. The section and seat inventory, approved drawings, work packages, final
   completion records, and as-built details for stadium-seat replacement from
   2023 through the present. Please identify every seat or entire section whose
   stanchions, brackets, trays, spacing, row location, or attachment geometry
   changed, including the work described in the June 2026 provisional review
   as approximately 25 percent of seats throughout the ballpark.
8. Final drawings, structural calculations, survey or layout records, shop
   drawings incorporated into the record set, and completion records for the
   2025-2026 phase-two replacement of the Mariners Vision main LED board. The
   requested scope includes the 209-foot display, panels, support frame,
   attachments, catwalks, service platforms, fascia, and any geometry retained
   from the earlier board.
9. The final 2025 and 2026 annual capital plans, final ten-year plans,
   Maintenance and Operations Reviews, CAA ICON project-monitoring workpapers,
   project lists with location and completion status, and closeout or
   reimbursement packages for any project that changed seating or a potential
   shadow-casting structure.
10. The final accepted documents for any later project through the date of the
    response that altered a seating tier, concrete profile, structural steel
    member, roof component, canopy, overhang, soffit, fascia, press-level
    projection, scoreboard, or other permanent obstruction.

For every supplied drawing, model, survey, or point cloud, please preserve the
coordinate reference system, datum, realization, epoch, units, scale,
revision, issue status, and acceptance date. Native DWG, DGN, RVT, IFC,
LandXML, LAS, LAZ, E57, CSV, or other machine-readable files are preferred.
Original PDF record drawings are also requested. Marketing renderings and
screenshots are not substitutes for metric records.

If a requested record is held by the Seattle Mariners, CAA ICON, Populous,
Skanska, the roof manufacturer, a surveyor, or another contractor on the PFD's
behalf, please identify the custodian and the contractual record location. If
any record is withheld or redacted, please identify the record and the specific
legal basis.

## Draft B: Seattle Department of Construction and Inspections

Subject: Public disclosure request for T-Mobile Park permit plans and final
inspection records

Please provide a permit and project index for T-Mobile Park, 1250 First Avenue
South, and electronic copies of responsive issued permits, approved plans,
revisions, structural calculations, deferred submittals, inspection records,
certificates of completion, and final or record drawings for:

- the 2022-2023 Press Club, Diamond Club, and working press-box projects;
- stadium seating replacement from 2023 through the present;
- the 2025-2026 Mariners Vision main LED replacement and its support frame;
- retractable-roof structural, track, control-position, or repowering work;
- structural-steel, concrete, deck, canopy, overhang, soffit, fascia, catwalk,
  or scoreboard work that changed physical geometry; and
- any project since the original stadium construction that changed a ticketed
  section, row, seating tier, or structure capable of casting a shadow into
  spectator seating.

Please include related record numbers and project addresses when a project was
filed under a different stadium address. Native electronic files should be
provided when held. If approved plan copies require a separate individually
written request to the SDCI Microfilm Library, please identify every relevant
permit number and route or return the responsive approved plan set.

This request does not seek security-system, access-control, camera, emergency,
or life-safety plans unless a responsive sheet is necessary to define the
exterior geometry of a requested seating or shadow-casting structure. Those
unrelated sheets may be omitted.

## Fee and delivery boundary

Electronic delivery is preferred. Before incurring any fee, please provide an
itemized estimate and identify the records proposed for withholding or
redaction. No fee is authorized by this draft.

## Required handling after receipt

Receiving records will not by itself make T-Mobile Park publication-ready.
Every file must be checksum-locked, dated, visually reviewed, tied to the
current configuration, and registered to surveyed horizontal and vertical
control. Row and obstruction coverage must reach 100%, uncertainties must pass
the one-foot and one-degree gates, and separate roof states must be modeled.
The resulting versioned geometry must then pass 30 independent, timestamped
shadow holdouts across at least three dates and at least 25 degrees of solar
altitude span.
