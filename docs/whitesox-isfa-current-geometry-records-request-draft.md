## Subject

Rate Field current seating-bowl, row, canopy, roof, and shadow-casting obstruction geometry records

## Draft request

To the Illinois Sports Facilities Authority FOIA Officer:

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act, I request electronic copies of the following existing records for Rate Field, 333 West 35th Street, Chicago. ISFA identifies itself as the owner and developer of the facility.

1. The most recent record architectural and structural as-built drawing sets for the seating bowl, including plans, sections, elevations, and details that show seating-row treads, risers, vomitories, cross aisles, decks, suites, clubs, railings, canopies, roofs, and overhangs.
2. The most recent surveyed site plan or building-control plan that ties the stadium to a stated horizontal coordinate system, true north, vertical datum, benchmarks, and permanent control points.
3. Current CAD, BIM, or other native digital geometry corresponding to items 1 and 2, including referenced files and coordinate-system documentation.
4. Seating-bowl schedules or dimensioned details that map public section and row identifiers to physical row treads or elevations.
5. Current drawings and shop drawings for shadow-casting structures facing the seating bowl or playing field, including scoreboards, ribbon boards, video displays, sign cabinets, light standards, canopy edges, roof edges, press or camera structures, and fixed outfield structures.
6. Record drawings, approved submittals, or closeout drawings for geometry changes completed from January 1, 2022 through the date of the search, including the Rate Field naming and signage work, the two-level flagship store at Gate 5, and projects described in ISFA materials as fiscal-year 2026 renovations.
7. A current drawing index, BIM model index, or facilities record inventory sufficient to identify the revision date and superseded status of the records responsive to items 1 through 6.
8. Any survey, scan, photogrammetry, point-cloud, or dimensional-verification report commissioned for the current facility from January 1, 2022 through the date of the search.

The purpose of the request is to calculate seat-level solar exposure and structural shadows accurately. I am not requesting security-system plans, access-control details, network diagrams, emergency-response procedures, or other operational security information. If responsive drawings contain exempt security information, please redact only that information and release the reasonably segregable architectural and dimensional content.

Please provide native electronic files when feasible. PDF is acceptable for drawing sheets, and DWG, DXF, RVT, IFC, point-cloud, spreadsheet, or other maintained native formats are requested for digital geometry and schedules. Please include referenced files needed to interpret a model or drawing.

If the complete current record set is too large for immediate production, please first release the current drawing and model indexes, surveyed site or control plan, seating-bowl plans and sections, and the current roof, canopy, scoreboard, and display drawings. A secure electronic download is acceptable.

Please provide a written fee estimate before incurring any charge. No fee, physical-media purchase, or paid reproduction is authorized by this draft.

Please make records available on a rolling basis if that would accelerate production.
