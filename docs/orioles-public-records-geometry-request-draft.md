# UNSENT DRAFT: Oriole Park metric geometry and control records request

Status: draft only. Not submitted.

This draft is intentionally limited to records needed to validate exact
row-level sun and shade calculations. It does not authorize submission,
payment, account creation, or disclosure of personal contact details.

## Proposed recipients

1. Baltimore City Department of Transportation, TEC Division, Survey Section
2. Maryland Department of Information Technology, MD iMAP imagery program
3. Maryland Stadium Authority, Public Information Act coordinator

Separate submissions are preferable because each custodian controls a
different evidence layer.

## Draft A: Baltimore Survey Section control records

Subject: Request for current survey control cards and accuracy metadata near
Oriole Park at Camden Yards

Please provide the current control card, recovery history, coordinate-system
definition, adjustment information, survey order or accuracy classification,
and numeric horizontal and vertical uncertainty for the following Baltimore
City Survey Section monuments:

- X501, Charles Street at Getting Avenue, map category CURRENT
- X759, Washington Boulevard at Carey Street, map category CURRENT
- X843, Covington Street at Grindall Street, map category CURRENT
- X756, corresponding to NGS PID AI8098
- X773, corresponding to NGS PID AI8100
- X497, corresponding to NGS PID AI8083

For each point, please include:

- the complete front and back of the current control card;
- the point's current monument description and most recent recovery date;
- the precise horizontal datum, datum realization, coordinate epoch, map
  projection, and units for every published X/Y or easting/northing value;
- the vertical datum, geoid model, epoch, and units for elevation;
- the adjustment name and date;
- the point's survey order or numeric 95% horizontal and vertical uncertainty;
- any superseded coordinates, destroyed or disturbed status, and replacement
  monument references; and
- any available digital coordinate export for the requested points.

The public Survey Control Points web map identifies these monuments but does
not include the point-specific scans, verification values, uncertainty, datum
realization, or adjustment epoch needed for a defensible metric registration.

Preferred format: original PDF or TIFF card scans plus CSV, TXT, SHP, GeoJSON,
or geodatabase coordinates. Native files are preferred over screenshots.

## Draft B: 2025 Maryland three-inch imagery quality records

Subject: Request for 2025 Baltimore City three-inch imagery accuracy and frame
lineage records

Please provide the final quality-control and delivery records for the 2025
three-inch aerial imagery covering Oriole Park at Camden Yards, centred near
39.2838, -76.6218. Requested records include:

- the final horizontal positional-accuracy report, including the confidence
  definition, RMSE values, NSSDA values, checkpoint count, checkpoint
  distribution, residuals, and units;
- the aerial-triangulation report and final bundle-adjustment summary;
- the ground-control and independent-checkpoint coordinate tables with datum,
  realization, epoch, projection, units, and point uncertainty;
- the camera calibration report for the acquisition camera;
- final exterior-orientation records, original frame imagery, and exact frame
  footprints for standard-overlap frames 3113-84719, 3113-84720, 3114-84789,
  and 3114-84790;
- the same records for high-overlap frames 23003-80081 through 23003-80085,
  23004-80114 through 23004-80118, 23005-80145 through 23005-80149,
  23006-80178 through 23006-80182, and 23007-80209 through 23007-80213;
- seamline, cutline, or source-image lineage identifying which raw frame or
  frames contribute pixels to orthophoto tile 45088308 at the stadium;
- original delivered source tile `45088308.tif` and any associated auxiliary,
  world, projection, metadata, or checksum files;
- the exact acquisition date and time for the stadium imagery, including
  clarification of the high-overlap records' `03102025` source-layer suffix
  because that layer description omits March 10, and the standard-overlap
  records' `03122025` suffix because that layer description omits March 12; and
- any tile-specific accuracy, resampling, or orthorectification metadata.

Preferred format: original PDF reports, CSV or text adjustment outputs, native
GIS seamline or footprint layers, and original calibration files. A statement
that pixel size equals positional accuracy would not answer this request.

## Draft C: Maryland Stadium Authority current as-built records

Subject: Maryland Public Information Act request for current Oriole Park
as-built geometry

Please provide final as-built drawings, record drawings, survey files, and
native CAD or BIM models for the 2025-2026 first-phase renovations at Oriole
Park at Camden Yards, including:

- the center-field video board, its complete support frame, foundations, and
  attached equipment;
- the PureWager Pavilion and every canopy, roof, column, fascia, railing, and
  wall below the center-field video board;
- the Truist Club work in the former press-box area, including the seating-bowl
  facade, glazing, outdoor edge, ceiling, roof, and structural supports;
- the renovated Club Level bars along the first-base and third-base lines;
- the right-field wall display and its supports;
- Club Level ribbon boards, out-of-town boards, and their supports;
- the Corona Flag Court wraparound bar, railings, lighting supports, canopies,
  and other permanent fixtures; and
- any other completed 2025-2026 capital work that changed a surface capable of
  casting a shadow into a ticketed seating row.

For every drawing or model, please include the coordinate reference system,
horizontal and vertical datum, realization, epoch, units, survey control,
revision history, issue status, and final acceptance date. Native DWG, DGN,
RVT, IFC, LandXML, point-cloud, or other machine-readable files are preferred.
PDF record drawings are also requested.

Please also provide the current seating-bowl record set for ticketed seating,
including row centerlines or tread and riser geometry, section boundaries,
aisle geometry, deck elevations, fascia, catwalks, roofs, overhangs, and
overhang undersides. Where renovations reused existing surfaces, please provide
the latest verified record drawing or survey that establishes the retained
geometry.

The requested records are needed to determine physical sun exposure at seat
row scale. Marketing renderings, photographs without camera calibration, and
general floor plans cannot establish the absolute coordinates, elevations,
undersides, or completeness required for this use.
